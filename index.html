🫁 Breathing reset

▾

4-4-6 breathing, five rounds. Follow the ring.

Ready
5 rounds · 4-4-6

💡 HALT

▾

Ask what is really driving the urge. Usually it is one of four things, and three of them have a simple fix.

H
Hungry. Eat something now. Low blood sugar reads as craving.
A
Angry. Name it out loud. Do not make a decision while it is running.
L
Lonely. This is the dangerous one. Contact a person, not a screen.
T
Tired. Sleep is not avoidance. It is maintenance.

🌊 Urge surfing

▾

The urge is a wave. It rises, peaks, then falls — every time. You do not have to fight it. You have to outlast it.

🧠 SOBER space

▾

A three-minute pause for high-risk moments.

S
Stop. Whatever you were about to do, pause it.
O
Observe. What is happening in your body right now?
B
Breathe. Ten slow breaths. Nothing else.
E
Expand. Widen your awareness. You are more than this moment.
R
Respond. Choose the next move deliberately, not automatically.

🗣 Scripts for real situations

▾

You don't need to explain yourself. You just need the words.

When someone offers
"No thanks, I'm good." Full stop. No explanation needed.
When they push
"I said I'm good. Let's talk about something else."
When you need to leave
"I've got an early start. I'm heading out." Then leave. Don't negotiate.
When you need someone
"I'm struggling. I need you to stay on the phone with me."
